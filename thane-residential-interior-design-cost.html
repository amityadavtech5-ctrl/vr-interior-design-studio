    Frequently asked questions

    How is the turnkey renovation estimate calculated?

    The estimate multiplies your selected carpet area by the selected finish tier rate and applies a 5% logistics adjustment. It is an indicative planning estimate, not a final quotation and should be verified with a site visit.

    What affects the interior design cost?

    The displayed estimate changes with carpet area and the finish tier. Final project costs may also depend on the agreed scope, site conditions, materials, fittings, and custom requirements.

    How long can a renovation take?

    The calculator estimates 4–6 weeks for areas below 800 sq. ft., 6–8 weeks for areas from 800 to below 1,300 sq. ft., and 8–12 weeks for areas of 1,300 sq. ft. or more. Actual timelines vary with scope and site readiness.
